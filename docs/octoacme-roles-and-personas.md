# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designers

### Role Summary
UX Designers focus on user experience, interaction design, and usability. They research user needs, create prototypes, and validate designs to ensure the product is intuitive and meets user expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and analyze user behavior patterns
- Create wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes
- Define information architecture and user flows
- Conduct usability testing and gather feedback
- Collaborate with Product Managers on user stories and acceptance criteria
- Work with Developers to ensure design feasibility and implementation accuracy

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce user friction and improve task completion rates
- Ensure design consistency across the product

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with Product Managers and stakeholders
- Handoff sessions with Developers on implementation details
- User research readouts and usability testing summaries
- Collaborative design workshops and brainstorming sessions

### Typical Interactions
- **With Product Managers**: Collaborate on feature definition, validate user needs, and align design decisions with business goals
- **With Developers**: Review technical constraints, provide design specs, and validate implementation during development
- **With Business Analysts**: Understand requirements and process flows to inform design decisions
- **With Project Managers**: Communicate design timelines, dependencies, and resource needs

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical solutions. They gather requirements, document processes, and ensure that delivered solutions align with business objectives and user workflows.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business requirements and user needs
- Create process flow diagrams and business logic specifications
- Define functional requirements and user acceptance criteria
- Analyze data to support decision-making and measure outcomes
- Facilitate requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Validate solutions against business requirements

### Goals
- Ensure solutions deliver measurable business value
- Reduce ambiguity in requirements and acceptance criteria
- Improve alignment between business stakeholders and delivery teams

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and functional specifications
- Stakeholder interviews and workshops
- Business case presentations and ROI analysis
- Status updates on requirements validation and acceptance testing

### Typical Interactions
- **With Product Managers**: Refine product requirements and translate business goals into detailed specifications
- **With Developers**: Clarify functional requirements, answer technical questions about business logic
- **With UX Designers**: Provide context on business processes and workflows to inform design
- **With Project Managers**: Support scope definition, estimation, and change management

---

## DevOps Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers enable reliable, efficient, and secure software delivery. They build and maintain CI/CD pipelines, manage infrastructure, monitor system health, and work to automate operational tasks.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Manage infrastructure, cloud resources, and environment configurations
- Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting solutions
- Ensure security practices are integrated into deployment processes
- Troubleshoot production issues and support incident response
- Optimize build times, deployment frequency, and system reliability

### Goals
- Reduce deployment friction and time-to-production
- Maintain high system availability and performance
- Enable developers to deploy confidently and frequently

### Typical Communication
- Infrastructure and deployment runbooks
- Incident response updates and post-mortems
- Capacity planning and performance metrics
- Security and compliance reports

### Typical Interactions
- **With Developers**: Provide deployment support, troubleshoot build/deployment issues, and collaborate on infrastructure needs
- **With Project Managers**: Communicate deployment timelines, capacity constraints, and infrastructure dependencies
- **With QA/Testing**: Coordinate testing environments and deployment pipelines
- **At Release**: Execute deployments, monitor system health, and support rollback if needed
